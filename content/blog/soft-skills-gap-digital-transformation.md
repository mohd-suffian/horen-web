Every digital transformation project we've seen stall shares a common thread: it wasn't a technology problem. The ERP was configured correctly. The cloud infrastructure was sound. But adoption was low, communication broke down, and change resistance set in.

## The skills companies underestimate

Technical training budgets in Malaysia have grown consistently. Soft skills budgets have not kept pace. Yet the research is clear — emotional intelligence, communication, and change management are the deciding factors in whether digital projects succeed.

## What we see in the room

When we run leadership and communication programmes alongside technical training, the difference is measurable. Teams that understand how to present change, manage resistance, and collaborate across functions implement technology faster and with less friction.

## The programmes that move the needle

- Presentation and communication skills
- Project management fundamentals
- Managing organisational change
- Team building for mixed-skill groups
