Cloud adoption doesn't fail because of technology. It fails because people across the organisation — from IT to finance to operations — don't share a common understanding of what the cloud actually is.

## Why AZ-900 first?

The Azure Fundamentals certification (AZ-900) is often mistaken for a purely technical credential. In practice, it's one of the most effective alignment tools a company can invest in.

When your procurement team understands consumption-based pricing, your finance team can budget accurately. When your operations team understands availability zones, your IT team spends less time explaining downtime risks. AZ-900 creates that shared language.

## What the certification covers

- Core cloud concepts (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS)
- Azure architecture and services
- Azure pricing, SLAs, and support
- Security, compliance, and trust

## Who should take it

Everyone touching a cloud migration project — not just developers. We regularly run AZ-900 cohorts that include project managers, business analysts, and senior leaders alongside IT teams.
